João Segurado (1944 – 2018) was a Portuguese graphic designer. He studied at the Escola Superior de Belas Artes de Lisboa (ESBAL) and was a part of the generation of Portuguese graphic designers that studied graphic design at the Ravensbourne College of Art in London, together with <a class="text cat-link author" href="/authors/Alda&20Rosa/">Alda Rosa</a>, <a class="text cat-link author" href="/authors/José&20Pinto&20Nogueira/">José Pinto Nogueira</a>, and Cristina Reis. He worked as a designer for <strong>O Jornal</strong>, <strong>Jornal de Artes e Letras</strong>, and <strong>Imprensa de Ciências Sociais</strong>.
